Trick or Treat

Dress up your Mac's Desktop for Halloween.

Right-click any file or folder and choose 🎃 Trick or Treat. It gets a surprise costume, with a spooky sound to match.

Download for Mac

Free · macOS 13 Ventura or later · Apple silicon & Intel · 1.7 MB

How it works

  1. Right-click any file or folder on your Desktop and choose 🎃 Trick or Treat.
  2. It gets a random costume. Files keep their preview underneath, so a screenshot stays a screenshot, just wearing a witch hat.
  3. Halloween over? Right-click it and choose 🧹 Undecorate, or pick 🧹 Undecorate Desktop to clear everything at once. It all goes back exactly how it was.

Install

  1. Open the DMG and drag Trick or Treat into Applications.
  2. Open the app. If macOS says it can't verify it, click Done, go to System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. You only do this once.
  3. In the window that opens, click Open Settings and switch on the Trick or Treat Finder extension.
  4. Right-click something on your Desktop. Happy Halloween!
